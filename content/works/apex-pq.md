A university identity portal experiment built around post-quantum authentication.

## Highlights

- Single-use, relying-party-bound ML-DSA-44 login challenges.
- Chrome wallet with an encrypted key vault.
- Three SQLite replicas with a hash-chained ledger and 2-of-3 write quorum.

## Status

Research and teaching prototype. It is not audited or suitable for real accounts; its default demo mode performs signing on the server.

An architecture for combining classical and post-quantum authentication without hard-coding a single algorithm.

## Direction

- Hybrid signatures and key encapsulation.
- Pluggable algorithms, storage, and key-custody interfaces.
- Built around established cryptographic libraries, with no hand-rolled primitives.

## Status

Design stage: the repository contains specifications and planning documents, not released code. It is not audited or FIPS-validated.
